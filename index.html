CAPABILITIES & LIMITS

Bluetooth: On supported browsers, you can choose a nearby Bluetooth device. Some browsers also support experimental BLE scanning. The browser does not expose every device automatically.

GPS: Reports your phone's location only. It does not reveal GPS satellite direction or signal strength.

Wi-Fi / cellular / general RF: Mobile websites cannot scan Wi-Fi channels, identify cell towers, or measure arbitrary radio frequencies. Those require native software or external radio hardware.

Compass and marker: The cyan arrow shows the direction your phone is facing. The green marker records the compass heading at which the strongest RSSI sample was received. It is a search clue, not a measured transmitter bearing or physical location.
