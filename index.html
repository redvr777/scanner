CAPABILITIES & LIMITS

Bluetooth: On supported browsers, you can choose a nearby Bluetooth device. Some browsers also support experimental BLE scanning. The browser does not expose every device automatically.

GPS: Reports your phone's location only. It does not reveal GPS satellite direction or signal strength.

Compass: Uses your phone's magnetometer. The arrow at the top shows the direction you're facing (the back of the phone when held upright, the top edge when held flat). Calibrate by waving the phone in a figure-8 if it drifts.

Wi-Fi / cellular / general RF: Mobile websites cannot scan Wi-Fi channels, identify cell towers, or measure arbitrary radio frequencies. Those require native software or external radio hardware.

Direction of signals: The browser can't read a transmitter's true bearing. Instead, the app logs signal strength (RSSI) at each compass heading while you turn, and pins the device in the direction where it was strongest. Pins stay fixed to the world and move around the dial as you rotate. It is an estimate: turn slowly in a full circle, hold the phone steady, and expect walls and your body to skew it. Distance from center is a rough guide from signal strength.
