DIRECTION FINDING
1. Enable your compass.

2. Start Bluetooth scanning.

3. Slowly rotate your phone through a full circle.

4. Watch the signal strength as you turn.

5. The radar remembers the heading where the strongest reading was observed.

6. Move around and repeat to compare readings.
CAPABILITIES & LIMITS
Bluetooth RSSI measures received signal strength, not distance or bearing. The strongest-heading marker is a direction recorded when a strong signal was received, not proof that the transmitter is physically in that direction.

The browser can only detect Bluetooth devices that advertise and are accessible through its Bluetooth API. Wi-Fi, cellular and arbitrary RF scanning require other hardware or software.
